## Research

My current research is focused on **neurosymbolic learning** for **knowledge graphs**, ontologies, description logics and their domain-specific application in the railway engeneering. Specifically I'm studying how to **inject background knowledge** in knowledge graph embedding models, with the parallel task of evaluating the capabilities of the model to represent the learned semantics. In the past I have worked on computer vision for image classification and adversarial attacks, genetic optimization and quantum computing focusing in hybrid model for contrastive learning and clustering.
